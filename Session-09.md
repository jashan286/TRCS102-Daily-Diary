# Session 9: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to Exploratory Data Analysis.
- Understanding data patterns and distributions.
- Identifying relationships between variables.
- Data visualization techniques.

## Key Learnings

- **EDA:** The process of analyzing datasets to understand their structure, patterns, and relationships before building a model.
- **Data Visualization:** Representing data graphically to make patterns easier to understand.
- **Histograms:** Used to examine the distribution of numerical data.
- **Scatter Plots:** Help identify relationships between two numerical variables.
- **Correlation:** Measures the strength and direction of the relationship between numerical variables.
- **Outlier Detection:** Helps identify unusual observations that may require further investigation.

## Common Python Tools
- `df.describe()` – Generates statistical summaries.
- `df.corr()` – Calculates correlations between numerical columns.
- `Matplotlib` – Creates charts and graphs.
- `Seaborn` – Helps create statistical visualizations.

## Key Takeaways
- EDA helps identify patterns, trends, and unusual values.
- Visualizations make complex datasets easier to understand.
- Correlation helps examine relationships between numerical variables.
- EDA supports better feature selection and data preparation.

## Summary
Learned how to explore datasets using statistical summaries, correlation analysis, and visualizations to understand data patterns and prepare for Machine Learning.

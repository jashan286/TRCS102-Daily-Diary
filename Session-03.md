# Session 3: Real-World Applications and Use Cases of AI/ML

## Topics Covered
- Real-world applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- Applications of Machine Learning (ML).
- Benefits of AI/ML across different industries.

## Key Learnings

- **Healthcare:** AI/ML helps analyze medical images, predict diseases, and support diagnosis.
- **Education:** AI enables personalized learning and intelligent tutoring systems.
- **Finance:** ML is used for fraud detection, credit scoring, and risk analysis.
- **E-commerce:** AI recommends products based on customer interests and shopping history.
- **Transportation:** AI supports self-driving technology, traffic prediction, and route optimization.
- **Agriculture:** ML helps monitor crop health, predict yields, and detect plant diseases.

## Key Takeaways
- AI/ML improves efficiency and automates repetitive tasks.
- Machine learning identifies patterns in data to make predictions.
- AI/ML is widely used in healthcare, education, finance, and many other industries.
- Data quality and responsible use are important for reliable AI systems.

## Summary
Explored real-world applications of AI and ML across different industries and understood how these technologies help solve practical problems.

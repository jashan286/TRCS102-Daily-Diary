# Session 1: Introduction to AI, ML, and Deep Learning

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- Basics of Machine Learning (ML).
- Introduction to Deep Learning (DL).

## Key Learnings
- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI that allows computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions.
- **Deep Learning (DL):** A subset of ML that uses multi-layered neural networks to solve complex problems.

## Applications
- AI: Virtual assistants and smart robots.
- ML: Spam detection and price prediction.
- Deep Learning: Image recognition and speech processing.

## Key Takeaways
- AI is the broader field of intelligent machines.
- ML learns from data, while DL uses neural networks to learn complex patterns.
- These technologies are widely used in real-world applications.

## Summary
Learned the fundamental concepts of AI, ML, and Deep Learning, their differences, and their practical applications.
